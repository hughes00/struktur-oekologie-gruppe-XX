Scope, assumptions and open decisions

Quantities use analytical axis lengths and nominal cross-sections. Rectangular tapers are integrated assuming dimensions vary linearly; circular tubes exclude the hollow core. These quantities can differ from RFEM display Breps. The 3D view uses 561 unmodified Rhino solid meshes and generated rods for 175 axis-only members.

The 47 low-level Rectangle 500/500 members are assigned concrete C25/30 by rule; concrete grade remains an assumption. Steel density: 7,850 kg/m³. Foundation rebar: 85 kg/m³. Eighteen conflicting material labels stay unresolved at the user’s request. Human changes in the member passport are stored in this browser, exported with provenance, and never modify Rhino.

C means C1–C4. Undeclared modules are shown as missing, never silently zero. D is shown separately; it is not subtracted from A1–A3. A4, A5 and B are outside the assignment’s selected scope. Cached responses are timestamped snapshots, not live data. A refresh attempts all five specified public endpoints.
